Reporting Content

Each game card can be reported by long-pressing it (or right-clicking on desktop). This opens a report dialog with three options:

Title is inappropriate — Use this if the trend keyword itself is offensive, sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise unsuitable content.

Image is inappropriate — Use this if the image displayed on the card is offensive, disturbing, sexually explicit, or otherwise unsuitable regardless of its license status.

Image is not free use — Use this if you recognize the image as one that is not covered by a Creative Commons or equivalent license, or if you are the rights holder and have not granted permission for its use.

Reports are reviewed and acted upon by removing the offending content from the game's data. We aim to respond to reports promptly.

I'm Not A Robot Privacy Policy

June 2, 2026

I'm Not A Robot is a memory match game developed by Khan and ClayRobot Games. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account and does not identify you in any way.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves game progress, preferences (theme, sound, difficulty), and image validation results to your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is only used to remember your game state between sessions.

Network Requests

The app fetches daily trending topic data and associated images from our backend server to populate the game cards. These requests do not include any user identifiers or personal data — the server has no way to associate a request with a specific person or device.

Images displayed on cards are loaded directly from their original sources (Wikipedia and other public web sources).

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
